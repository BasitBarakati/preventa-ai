# PREVENTA AI — Product and Experience Reference

PREVENTA AI is an **AI-Enabled Public Health Transformation Platform**.

**Primary message:** From Public Health Intelligence to Practical Action.

**Supporting message:** Assess needs. Understand risks. Apply evidence. Build healthier communities.

## Product model

Every major capability connects three layers:

1. **Knowledge** — plain-language evidence, context, assumptions, and limitations.
2. **Tools** — assessments, dashboards, reports, assistants, workspaces, courses, and resources.
3. **Action** — accountable plans, referrals, programs, decisions, learning, and measurable next steps.

The shared workflow is **Assess → Understand → Act**. AI can organize evidence and support interpretation, but people remain accountable for consequential decisions.

## Five platform pillars

1. **Situation Assessment** — individual and family wellbeing, community needs and assets, and organizational culture and AI readiness.
2. **Health and AI Implementation** — responsible implementation across transformation, mental wellbeing, substance use, healthy environments, healthy sexuality, and immunization.
3. **Indigenous Health and Wellbeing** — community-governed, culturally safe pathways grounded in wholistic health, land, culture, and social determinants.
4. **AI in Research and Risk Prediction** — evidence generation, controlled research sandboxes, validation, bias and fairness, and auditable outputs.
5. **Courses and Workforce Learning** — applied public-health learning pathways, exercises, reflection, and certificate-ready course structures.

## Trust commitments

- Evidence-grounded responses with visible citations.
- Human review before consequential use.
- Privacy by design and data minimization.
- Role-based access, encryption, and audit logging in production workspaces.
- Clear intended-use boundaries and AI limitations.
- No diagnosis or individualized clinical advice.
- No real personal-health prediction in the public phase.
- Design that supports PHIPA- and PIPEDA-aligned implementation without claiming guaranteed compliance.

## Indigenous governance direction

Implementation must be relationship-based and community-governed. Product direction includes Indigenous co-design, an Indigenous Advisory Circle, OCAP principles, Two-Eyed Seeing, community data authority, cultural safety, and locally defined understandings of wholistic health, land, culture, and social determinants. PREVENTA AI does not speak on behalf of any Nation or community.

## Source instruments

The foundational documents in `docs/foundational/` remain source material for future validated assessment implementations. Digitization must preserve informed consent, anonymity or de-identification requirements, question-specific scales, voluntary participation, crisis and support pathways, and explicit non-diagnostic limitations.

## Public-preview boundary

The current website includes local-only interactions and clearly labelled interface previews. Authentication, live AI retrieval, research databases, learning-management features, report generation, email delivery, and sensitive-data workflows require secure backend integrations and formal governance review before activation.
